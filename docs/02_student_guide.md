# MajorCare student guide

## Creating an account

1. Open the MajorCare login page and click **Register**.
2. Enter your school ID, full name, and password (at least 8 characters). Email is optional.
3. Click **Submit registration**.

Your account is pending until clinic staff approve it. You cannot log in before approval. If staff reject your registration, the login page shows the reason.

## Logging in

Enter your school ID and password on the login page. If staff reset your password, you must enter the temporary password first, then choose a new one.

## The privacy notice

The first time you open a student page, MajorCare shows the privacy notice. Read it, tick the box, and click **Accept and continue**. You cannot use the other pages until you accept it.

## Your pages

| Page | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | See your next appointment, last visit, profile summary, and health summary |
| Profile | Update your contact details and emergency contact |
| Health | View your blood type, allergies, notes, and vaccination records. You cannot edit them. Contact the clinic to correct them. |
| Book | Choose an open time with seats left, give a reason, and submit a request |
| Appointments | View upcoming and past appointments. Cancel a pending or approved appointment before it starts. |
| Visits | View your clinic visit history. You cannot edit it. |
| Documents | Upload a PDF, JPG, or PNG up to 5 MB. Choose a type: Laboratory result, Physical examination form, Medical clearance, or Other. |
| Check-ups | View check-up results that the clinic has published |
| Messages | Send a message to clinic staff. Messages are for appointments, documents, and non-urgent concerns only. |
| Staff | See each clinic staff member's availability and contact method |
| Clinic | View the clinic's location, hours, contact details, published FAQs, and announcements |
| Notifications | View your notifications. Opening one marks it as read. |

## Documents

- Your document is marked **Pending** until clinic staff review it.
- **Approved** documents show an **Open document** link.
- **Rejected** documents show the reason staff gave. You can upload a corrected file.

## Check-ups

Staff record your check-up results, such as height, weight, BMI, and blood pressure. You see a result only after staff publish it. Results are read-only. To correct one, contact the clinic.

## Appointments

- A slot can have several students, up to its seat limit. The booking page shows how many seats are left.
- You can hold one seat per time. A second request for the same time is refused.
- Staff may reject or cancel a request, and you get a notification when they do.
- To cancel, open **Appointments** and click **Cancel appointment**. You cannot cancel after the start time.

## Notifications

Notifications appear in the top bar with a count. Open **Notifications** to see them. Opening an item marks it read. **Mark all as read** clears the rest.

## Things MajorCare does not do

- It does not diagnose conditions or give medical advice.
- It does not handle emergencies. In an emergency, go to the clinic or call emergency services.
- It does not let you edit your health records, visit records, or check-up results.
