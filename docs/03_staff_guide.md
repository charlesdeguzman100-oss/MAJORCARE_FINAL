# MajorCare staff guide

## Logging in

Use your staff ID and password on the login page. The first staff account is created during setup.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows:

- Approved students
- Pending registrations
- Pending appointment requests
- Upcoming approved appointments
- Visits recorded this month
- Documents awaiting review
- Unread student messages

Each card links to the page where you act on it.

## Registrations

Open **Registrations**. Each pending student shows their name, school ID, and email. Click **Approve**, or enter a reason and click **Reject**. Rejections need a reason.

## Students

Open **Students** to see every approved student. For each student you can:

- **View profile.** Read-only view of account, personal details, and emergency contact.
- **Health records.** Record blood type, allergies, notes, and vaccination records.
- **Visits.** Record a clinic visit, edit it, and view the history. Visit records are never deleted.
- **Check-ups.** Record a physical assessment as a draft, edit the draft, and publish it. Publish sends the student a notification. Unpublish returns it to draft if a correction is needed.
- **Messages.** Open the conversation from **Messages**.
- **Reset password.** Generates a temporary password. Give it to the student in person. The message with the password appears once. The student must change it at the next login.

## Appointments and slots

**Slots** lets you add a time slot with a date, start and end time, and the number of seats (1 to 50). You can:

- Close or reopen a slot.
- Change the number of seats. You cannot lower it below the number of seats already taken.
- Delete a slot, but only when it has no appointments.

**Appointments** lets you filter requests by status and act on them:

- **Approve** a pending request.
- **Reject** a pending request. A note is required.
- **Cancel** a pending or approved request. A note is required.
- **Mark completed** an approved request.

Rejected and cancelled requests release their seat.

## Documents

Open **Documents** and choose a filter. Pending is the default. Open a document to view it, then approve it or reject it with a note. Each document can be reviewed once.

## Messages

**Messages** lists every student conversation with a count of unread student messages. Open one to read it and reply. Opening a conversation marks its student messages as read.

## Availability

Open **Availability** to set your status (Available, Busy, or Unavailable) and the contact method students see. Your status is not changed automatically. If you never update it, students see Unavailable.

## Clinic information, FAQs, and announcements

- **Clinic** edits the clinic's name, location, hours, and contact details. This record is edited, never deleted.
- **FAQs** lets you add, edit, reorder, show or hide, and delete questions.
- **Announcements** lets you write, publish, unpublish, edit, and delete announcements. Publishing notifies every approved student. Editing and unpublishing do not send notifications.

## Notifications

Open **Notifications** for alerts about registrations, appointment requests, documents, check-up publishing, messages, and reminders. A reminder appears for each approved appointment that starts within 24 hours.

## Records that cannot be deleted

These records are corrected, not deleted:

- Visit records (correct them by editing)
- Check-up results (correct them by unpublishing, editing the draft, and publishing again)
- Clinic information (edit only)
